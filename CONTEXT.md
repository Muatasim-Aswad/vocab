# Vocabulary

Vocabulary records words and meanings for later study. A saved term may need review before it becomes a vocabulary entry.

## Language

**Entry**:
A vocabulary record for one meaning of a term. Its meaning may still need confirmation when it is a stub.

**Stub**:
An entry standing in for an unresolved relationship target. Its meaning has not been confirmed.

**Capture**:
A term deliberately saved for later review, before its meaning or relationship to existing entries is decided. A capture is not an entry.
_Avoid_: Pending entry

**Active entry**:
An entry accepted into the vocabulary for study.
